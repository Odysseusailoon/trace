Every paper we position against, with what it shows and how forkscope relates. The field is splitting into three branches — representation probes (cheap but indirect), engine-determinism audits (the methodological floor), and fork-as-training-signal (the application) — and the resampling measurement they all lean on is the part we build.

1 · The resampling lineage — our direct ancestors

Forking Paths in Neural Text Generation
Bigelow, Holtzman, Tanaka, Ullman · ICLR 2025
The Forking Tokens Hypothesis: individual sampled tokens can send a generation to a qualitatively different ending. Operationalized by position-wise resampling (FPA); finds surprise forks at punctuation. Dynamic uncertainty — invisible to any static readout of final-answer logits.
The origin of the ot object we measure. We port it from text to tool-using agents.
Forking Fast
Bigelow et al. · Aug 2026 · the paper we replicate directly
Makes FPA affordable. Resampling noise is exactly Multinomial(S, ot) with 1/√S decay, and at the distribution level low-S "forks" are consistent with that noise — some earlier forks were likely artifacts (their claim is distributional; they do not re-adjudicate individual tokens). PELT changepoints plus kernel-weighted Dirichlet pooling give a 4–15× effective-sample-size gain.
Our 8B results agree with the noise-dominates correction — decision forks are ~1% of MCQ probes, drift is the norm — and we push the method into the agent domain where the fork structure actually lives.
Thought Anchors: Which LLM Reasoning Steps Matter?
Bogdan, Macar, Nanda, Conmy · 2025
Raises the resampling unit from token to sentence: resample each sentence 100×, filter semantically equivalent replacements, watch the answer distribution. Anchors are mostly planning and backtracking sentences.
Same instrument family, CoT sentences instead of agent actions.
Thought Branches: Interpreting LLM Reasoning Requires Resampling
Macar, Bogdan, Rajamanoharan, Nanda · ICLR 2026
The methodological manifesto: one CoT is not enough for causal interpretation; study the prompt-induced distribution over trajectories. Adds resilience (how many resamples before a sentence's content disappears) and effect (causal impact when it is fully absent).
Closest interpretability work to ours. They sample CoT sentences over HF/API; we run tool trajectories natively in the serving engine, where prefix caching changes the economics — and the chat-template versus bare-continuation distribution mismatch we document does not arise in their setting.
Critical-token RL: Vassoyan et al. 2025 · Prototype Entropy Alignment (AAAI 2026)
Training-side corroboration
Both act on the same intuition from the training side: a small set of tokens disproportionately decides outcomes, so explore harder (or align entropy) exactly there.
Independent evidence for the premise; neither measures which tokens those are in deployed agents.

2 · Agent failure attribution — the application we compete in

Who&When
Zhang et al. · ICML 2025 Spotlight
Defines automated failure attribution: find the earliest agent step whose correction flips the outcome. 127 multi-agent failure logs; three LLM-judge strategies. The best locates the decisive step 14.2% of the time — o1 and R1 included.
The key contrast. Judges read logs and guess at 14%; replay measures the decisive step directly (t4: intdiv 46%→0% is a measured causal fact, not a judgment). Forkscope is the measurement-based solution to their task — costlier than a judge, but with ground truth.
AgenTracer — and the 2025–26 attribution family
RAFFLES · GraphTracer · StepFinder · FAMAS · Causal Agent Replay · span-level localization
Counterfactual replay for annotating failed trajectories, plus at least seven follow-ups in a year. All of it targets multi-agent log attribution.
The nearest method neighbor — we must draw the line sharply when writing. Nobody in the family does distributional resampling of a single agent's trajectory (ot curves, failure-shape taxonomy). That is our slot.

3 · Credit assignment and PRMs — the same mathematical object, used to train

VinePPO
McGill / Mila · ICML 2025
PPO's value network is near-random on reasoning tasks; replace it with Monte-Carlo value — fork K continuations at every prefix, average the outcome reward. Beats PPO on MATH/GSM8K at 3× less wall-clock.
Mathematically, our ot[correct] is their V(st). They spend it on gradients, we spend it on diagnosis — and RadixAttention makes vine-style sampling cheap, a serving layer they never touched.
The PRM family: Math-Shepherd · OmegaPRM · PURE · PRISM · the 2026 survey
MC step labels → tree expansion → min-form credit → overcredit bias → 47-method taxonomy
Math-Shepherd's step label ("how many continuations from here answer correctly") is a discrete ot. PRISM documents the overcredit failure: PRMs score plausible-but-wrong steps highly.
Forkscope is the PRM annotation pipeline turned into a serving-native observability tool. PRISM's overcredit problem does not apply — we train no reward model, we measure.

4 · Recursive self-improvement — the narrative layer

Darwin Gödel Machine
Zhang et al., Sakana / Clune · ICLR 2026
Frozen weights, evolving scaffold: the agent reads its own eval logs, edits its own code, keeps what empirically works (archive + novelty bonus). SWE-bench 20%→50% over 80 rounds — with documented reward hacking (fabricated test logs).
DGM's self-improvement signal is "read the eval log" — the exact step Who&When shows runs at 14% accuracy. Measured fork evidence is the stronger signal to close that loop with, and a resampling measurement is external: the agent cannot talk it into a better score without changing the sampler.
Ecosystem: Red Queen Gödel Machine · MetaSkill-Evolve · AI4AI-Bench · statistical limits
Co-evolving evaluators · evolvable failure diagnosis · benchmarks · no-guarantee results
Every RSI loop in this ecosystem contains a "diagnose failures" step, and today every one of them implements it as an LLM judge reading logs. MetaSkill-Evolve evolves the diagnosis skill but keeps the diagnostic procedure itself fixed — complementary to, not competing with, an external instrument.
Forkscope is a drop-in measurement replacement for that one step. Minimal demo already run: fork report in, tool-description patch out, trap rate re-measured (§5 of the notes).

5 · Schema-induced SQL errors — precedent check for the t4 finding

ErrorLLM — and the self-correction ceiling
Corruption studies · execution-feedback limits
Ask an LLM to "check and fix" a correct SQL and it will obediently break it — prompt context itself inducing errors. And execution feedback only surfaces ~3% of wrong SQL (syntax); logic errors that run clean are invisible to every self-correction pipeline.
Our intdiv trap returns 1.000 with no error, and self-repair is 0% — now with a literature explanation. Schema wording that primes a specific wrong construction (Album.ArtistId → COUNT(DISTINCT ArtistId) integer division) has no direct precedent; schema-as-adversarial-context could stand alone as a short paper.

6 · Reading forks from the inside — mech-interp and critical tokens (added 2026-09-02)

Are language models aware of the road not taken?
Zur, Geiger, Lubana, Bigelow · Nov 2025 · Stanford / Goodfire / Harvard / NTT
Does the hidden state already encode ot for free? Three results on Llama-3.2-3B: steering success tracks ot dynamics (same changepoints; steering dies to ~0 after the fork commits); a linear probe on ht predicts ot far better than a probe on another model reading the same text (KL 0.11 vs 0.19) — the representation carries private, beyond-the-text information about where the model will go; correlation is moderate (R≈0.6), scale is tiny (14 cases, one 3B model, MCQ outcomes).
Three consequences for us: conceptual backing (a fork is "not yet committed"); their probe is a candidate resample-free screener — but training the agent version needs replay ground truth, which is what our instrument produces; and "steering fails after commit" is a hard timing constraint on any RSI patch: intervene at or before the decision point, the window closes after.
A Few Neurons Reveal When LLMs Misuse Tools
Ke et al. · Aug 2026
Sparse neurons detect agent tool misuse; selective steering corrects it.
Same problem domain, neuron route. Our SAE probe (§5 of the notes) is the feature-level cousin.
Fork concepts entering training: STARE · CADENCE · ACToR · CPR
Entropy-critical tokens in GRPO · forking-token boost in distillation · critical-token retrieval · critical-token routing
CADENCE names its gradient-concentration mechanism the "forking-token boost". The concept has moved from explanation to training signal in under a year.
The training-side convergence of our "forks as self-improvement signal" story.
The Agentic Garden of Forking Paths
Miao, Pritchard, Zou · 2026 · name collision, different concept
Researcher degrees of freedom in scientific analysis (Gelman's forking paths), not token forks.
Cite carefully; the overlap is the title.

7 · The methodological audit we must pass first

Re-feeding Is Not Replaying: Measuring Replay Noise in Counterfactual Token-Credit Estimation
Matteson · Jun 2026 · read before trusting any replay number, ours included
The standard replay implementation re-feeds the prefix as a fresh prompt. On stock engines that differs from resuming the decode-time KV state by 14–28pp of credit at low-margin decision points, and single-sample credit measurements are 9–23% non-reproducible there. Batch-invariant kernels make all three passes agree.
Direct hit on our method's core, and the strongest objection we have to clear: our replay re-feeds prefixes into the cache under SGLang deterministic inference — we need the replica floor under that setting, or a demonstration that re-feed ≡ resume there (queued as follow-up 4 in the notes). It also gives Forking Fast's "some forks are noise" a systems-level mechanism, and makes engine determinism itself a contribution.
